Clicking Through Feelings

Exhibition curated by Rayn Aziz

Curatorial Text

This exhibition shows how net art makes people interact with the artwork instead of just looking at it. You have to click, explore, or enter something, and that changes what you see. That is what makes it different from normal art.

All of these works use the internet as part of the artwork, not just a place to show it. We Feel Fine uses real emotions from people online and turns them into something visual. The Wilderness Downtown uses your location to make the experience feel personal. Superbad lets users explore and click through different pages.

What connects all of them is interaction. If you do not click or move around, the artwork does not really work. The viewer becomes part of the experience.
